01 / FEATURED PROJECT

Driver Drowsiness Detection.

Analyzing signs of driver drowsiness from video and camera input.

The Python project combines OpenCV-based face and eye detection with TensorFlow/Keras-based eye-state classification to explore signs of drowsiness.

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • TensorFlow/Keras
Project overview

The idea: explore signs of drowsiness by analyzing whether the driver's eyes are open or closed.

The approach: use OpenCV for face and eye detection, with a TensorFlow/Keras CNN for eye-state classification. The repository also includes video and camera analysis workflows.
